Proposal category dominates prediction; institutional identity nearly vanishes after controlling for proposal type. Climate (+6.1) and Human rights (+5.0) carry the largest log-odds toward dissent — what is being voted on matters far more than who is voting. Note: ESG coefficients may be inflated (quasi-separation in high-dissent cells).

Vanguard's raw 8.05% dissent rate appears 1.84× BlackRock's 4.37%. But 47.5% of Vanguard's dissents are WITHHOLD votes, not AGAINST. Stripping WITHHOLD from all three institutions collapses the ratio to 1.03× (BR 4.11%, SS 4.46%, VG 4.23%) — apparent divergence, not real divergence.

AUC 0.705, balanced accuracy 66.3% on 1.88M votes. Sample note: these are specific fund registrants (iShares Trust, SSIT/SPDR, Vanguard Index/World) — not full fund families.
