What the model tells us

Proposal category dominates prediction. A say-on-pay vote carries a -0.984 log-odds shift toward alignment — institutions almost always side with management on exec pay.

Climate and human rights push the opposite way. BlackRock (+0.366) adds incremental contrarian signal beyond category alone.

An AUC of 0.798 from only 29 features suggests voting behaviour is systematic and predictable.
